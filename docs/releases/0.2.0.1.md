# Sidee 0.2.0.1

Fix release: every built-in app tile now has a real icon. This completes the
logo work for the 20 presets added in 0.2.0.0, which shipped with broken or
missing tile images.

## What changed

- All 20 new presets (14 anime sites, 6 media aggregators) now have a bundled
  dashboard icon under `assets/logos/` and a working TV tile image.
- 8 icons are the sites' own logos, downloaded from the sites themselves:
  EverythingMoe, Wotaku, Miruro, Re:ANIME, AnimeX, AniPM, Cinejoy, Anidap.
- 12 icons are original wordmarks generated for Sidee, used where a site
  exposes no downloadable logo file: MKissa, NekoWatch, Anistream, Movy,
  PopcornMovies, FlyStream, Rive, 7Movies, The Index, JustIndex, AniKoto,
  AnimePahe.
- The TV tile image for The Index, JustIndex, and AniKoto still points at the
  site's own logo link; AnimePahe's tile image points at its generated
  wordmark because the site's own link stays behind a Cloudflare block.

## TV remote navigation on the new tiles

The three original apps (Nuvio, Stremio, Jellyfin) are TV-built: a dedicated
VIDAA page, a VIDAA build with D-pad navigation, and a TV client UI. The 20
new tiles are desktop websites, not TV builds. Sidee installs every tile the
same way (app id, name, URL, tile image) — there is no remote setting in the
install — so how far the TV remote gets inside each new tile depends on that
site: pages built for a mouse may not move focus with the arrow keys, and
video controls that need mouse hover may not appear at all. If a tile opens
but the remote cannot drive it, that is the site, not the install.

## Known limits (unchanged from 0.2.0.0)

- EverythingMoe's own logo is only 48x48, and The Index's tile image is
  served at 64px wide, so those two tiles look soft on a large TV.
- Miruro's and Re:ANIME's bundled logos are the seasonal Halloween variants
  the sites were serving when the logos were collected.
- AnimePahe has no real logo of its own (its site serves a joke placeholder
  file); its tile is a generated wordmark.
- Phone-side TV pairing (SSDP discovery, MQTT/TLS + PIN) is still unproven
  and needs a VIDAA TV or a simulated TV on the LAN to verify.

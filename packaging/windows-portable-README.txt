Sidee for Windows (portable, no Python needed)

Double-click Sidee.exe. It opens the Sidee dashboard in your browser.
Keep Sidee's window open while you use it; press Ctrl+C in that window
to stop Sidee when you are done.

If Sidee fails to start, read the error in its window and in Sidee.log
next to Sidee.exe, and paste the last lines of that log when reporting
the problem.

Safe mode: double-click Sidee-SafeMode.bat instead. It runs the same
dashboard without auto-opening a browser; copy the URL printed in the
console into your browser yourself.

Your TV pairing is saved in your user profile, not in this folder, so
you can move or replace this folder without re-pairing.
